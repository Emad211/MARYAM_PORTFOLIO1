**Verification status:** All numeric values below were taken from primary/official documents retrieved in August 2026 unless explicitly marked **[derived]** or **[university-dependent]**. Derived values are arithmetic consequences of cited official numbers.

## (a) Goethe-Zertifikat B1

*Provider:* Goethe-Institut (exam jointly developed with ÖSD and University of Fribourg). CEFR B1. Four modules; each module can be taken alone or combined; paper-based, digital (in-centre), and online formats share identical structure and scoring [S1].

### Modules [S1]

| Module | Parts | Items / tasks | Duration | Points | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 5 (Teil 1–5) | 30 items, each scored 1/0 | 65 min (incl. ~5 min answer transfer) | raw × 3.33 → 0–100 | 25 % of the four-module set |
| Hören | 4 (Teil 1–4) | 30 items, each scored 1/0 | ca. 40 min (incl. ~5 min transfer) | raw × 3.33 → 0–100 | 25 % |
| Schreiben | 3 (Teil 1–3) | 3 writing tasks | 60 min | max 100 raw (criteria-based) | 25 % |
| Sprechen | 3 (Teil 1–3) | 3 speaking tasks | pair ca. 15 min / individual ca. 10 min (+15 min prep) | max 100 (criteria-based) | 25 % |

Written total without breaks: **ca. 165 min**; ≥15-min break between written modules [S1].

### Scoring system [S1]
- Lesen/Hören: each item = 1 "Messpunkt"; result multiplied by 3.33 and rounded → module score out of 100 (e.g., 18/30 → 60).
- Schreiben/Sprechen: graded by criteria only at predefined point values (no intermediate values); max 100 per module.
- Writing/speaking double-rated by two independent assessors; scores averaged and rounded (≥0.5 up). Third rater if one rating is above and one below the 60-point pass line and the mean falls below 60.
- **Pass rule: each module independently needs ≥ 60/100. No compensation between modules; there is no aggregate overall score — the certificate lists all four module scores.**
- Grade bands per module: sehr gut 90–100 · gut 80–89 · befriedigend 70–79 · ausreichend 60–69 · nicht bestanden < 60.
- Failed modules can be retaken individually, any number of times where centre capacity allows.

### Speaking format [S1]
Pair exam by default (random pairing); individual exam in exceptional cases (examiner takes interlocutor role). Prep time 15 min (notes allowed). Structure: Teil 1 plan something together (~2–3 min); Teil 2 short presentation on a topic, choice of two topics (~3–4 min per candidate); Teil 3 feedback + questions from partner and second examiner (~1–2 min per candidate). Intro talk (~1 min) unassessed. Two examiners, both rate independently.

### Recognition
General-purpose B1 proof (residence, citizenship-track, work); below the standard German university direct-admission level — not among the certificates universities list for full Hochschulzugang [S12].

## (b) Goethe-Zertifikat B2

*Provider:* Goethe-Institut. CEFR B2. Four modular units, individually combinable; print/digital identical [S2].

### Modules [S2]

| Module | Parts | Items / tasks | Duration | Points | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 5 (Teil 1–5) | 30 items, 1/0 scoring | 65 min (incl. ~5 min transfer) | raw × 3.33 → 0–100 | 25 % |
| Hören | 4 (Teil 1–4) | 30 items, 1/0 scoring | ca. 40 min (incl. ~5 min transfer) | raw × 3.33 → 0–100 | 25 % |
| Schreiben | 2 (Teil 1–2) | 2 writing tasks | 75 min | max 100 raw (criteria-based) | 25 % |
| Sprechen | 2 (Teil 1–2) | presentation + discussion | pair ca. 15 min / individual ca. 10 min (+15 min prep) | max 100 (criteria-based) | 25 % |

Written total without breaks: **ca. 180 min**; ≥15-min breaks between written modules [S2].

### Scoring system [S2]
Identical mechanics to B1: item scaling ×3.33 for Lesen/Hören; criteria-based 0–100 for Schreiben/Sprechen; two independent raters averaged; third-rater rule at the 60-point boundary.
- **Pass rule: ≥ 60/100 in every one of the four modules; no compensation; no aggregate score.**
- Same grade bands as B1 per module. Individual module resits allowed.

### Speaking format [S2]
Pair exam default, individual possible. Prep 15 min with notes allowed. Teil 1: short presentation (choice of two topics), ~4 min per candidate, then questions from partner + examiner; Teil 2: joint discussion of a controversial topic, ~5 min for both together. Two examiners rate independently.

### Recognition
Widely required for employment/visa purposes and by some universities for preparatory programs; standard direct-admission proofs are C1/C2-level (see §d/e and [S12]).

## (c) Goethe-Zertifikat C1

*Provider:* Goethe-Institut. CEFR C1. Four modular units; print/digital identical [S3]. Note: the edition in force since 01.09.2025 changed durations vs. the pre-2025 format (written now 65/40/75 instead of 70/40/80).

### Modules [S3]

| Module | Parts | Items / tasks | Duration | Points | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 4 (Teil 1–4) | 30 items, 1/0 scoring | 65 min (incl. ~5 min transfer) | raw × 3.33 → 0–100 | 25 % |
| Hören | 4 (Teil 1–4) | 30 items, 1/0 scoring | ca. 40 min (incl. ~5 min transfer) | raw × 3.33 → 0–100 | 25 % |
| Schreiben | 2 (Teil 1–2) | 2 writing tasks | 75 min | max 100 raw (criteria-based) | 25 % |
| Sprechen | 2 (Teil 1–2) | presentation + discussion | pair ca. 20 min / individual ca. 15 min (+20 min prep) | max 100 (criteria-based) | 25 % |

Written total without breaks: **ca. 180 min**; ≥15-min breaks between written modules [S3].

### Scoring system [S3]
Same system as B1/B2: ×3.33 scaling for Lesen/Hören; criteria-based 0–100 for Schreiben/Sprechen; two raters averaged; third-rater rule at the 60-point line.
- **Pass rule: ≥ 60/100 per module, all four required; no compensation; no aggregate score.**
- Same grade bands per module.

### Speaking format [S3]
Pair exam default, individual possible. Prep 20 min with notes allowed. Teil 1: short presentation per candidate (~7 min each incl. questions from partner and an examiner); Teil 2: joint discussion of a controversial topic (~5 min both together). Two examiners rate independently.

### Recognition
Accepted by a number of universities as C1-level proof, but acceptance is institution-specific; many universities list Goethe C2 (GDS) rather than C1 for direct admission [S12]. Always check the target university's list.

## (d) telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule

*Provider:* telc gGmbH. CEFR C1, purpose-built for university access ("Hochschulzugang"). Written + oral block; available paper-based, digital, hybrid; individual subtests can be sat separately [S4].

### Modules [S4][S5]

| Subtest | Parts | Items / tasks | Duration | Points | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leseverstehen | 3 (T3 has 2 sub-tasks) | T1 text reconstruction 6×2 pts; T2 selective matching 6×2 pts; T3a detail R/F/not-in-text 11×2 pts; T3b heading match 1×2 pts → 24 decisions | shares a **90-min block with Sprachbausteine** (free split; ~15 min for answer transfer) | 48 | 22.5 % |
| Sprachbausteine (grammar/lexicon/spelling) | 1 | 22 gap-fill items (4 options each), 1 pt each | within the same 90-min block (recommended ~25 min) | 22 | 10 % |
| Hörverstehen | 3 | T1 global matching 8×1 pt; T2 detail MC 10×2 pts; T3 note-taking from lecture 10×2 pts → 28 items | 40 min incl. 5 min transfer (audio-paced) | 48 | 22.5 % |
| Schriftlicher Ausdruck | 1 | 1 essay ~350 words, choice of 2 topics; 4 criteria (Aufgabengerechtheit, Korrektheit, Repertoire, Kommunikative Gestaltung) | 70 min | 48 | 22.5 % |
| **Written total** | | | **200 min + 20-min break** | **166** | **77.5 %** |
| Mündlicher Ausdruck | 2 (T1A/T1B/T2) | presentation 3 min + summary/questions 2 min + quote discussion 6 min | 16 min pair (single/trio possible at odd numbers) + 20 min prep | 48 (content 16: 6+4+6; language 32: 8×4 criteria) | 22.5 % |
| **Whole exam** | | | | **214** | **100 %** |

Point/weighting figures from the official telc scoring table [S5]; durations/oral format from the current official exam page [S4] (identical to [S5]).

### Scoring system
- Objective parts: fixed points per item as listed above [S5].
- Essay and speaking: rated A–D per criterion, converted to fixed point values [S5].
- **Pass rule: written block and oral block are passed separately, each at ≥ 60 % of its own block total; no compensation between blocks; within the written block the four subtests compensate each other** [S6].
  - Written threshold **[derived]**: 60 % × 166 ≈ **100 points**.
  - Oral threshold **[derived]**: 60 % × 48 ≈ **29 points**.
- ⚠️ Data-quality note: some third-party prep sites state different totals (e.g., "240 written / 75 oral" on germanexam.pro). These contradict telc's own published scoring table [S5] and must not be used.
- Certificate shows an overall grade (sehr gut … ausreichend) but universities normally require only a pass [S6].

### Speaking format [S4][S5]
Paired exam (usually 2 candidates; single or trio exams possible), 20 min preparation. Teil 1A presentation (~3 min), Teil 1B partner's summary + follow-up questions (~2 min), Teil 2 discussion of a quotation (~6 min). Focus on candidate-to-candidate interaction; assessed on 5 criteria (Aufgabengerechtheit, Flüssigkeit, Repertoire, grammatische Richtigkeit, Aussprache/Intonation).

### Recognition
Recognized specifically as a university-admission certificate; listed alongside DSH-2 and TestDaF 4×4 in universities' accepted-proof lists (example: Uni Marburg) [S12]. Positioning vs. alternatives — TestDaF: centralized, globally sat before arrival, TDN 4 in all four sections ≈ DSH-2 equivalence; DSH: university-internal exam usually taken in Germany after conditional admission; telc C1 HS: external standardized alternative to both, same admission function.

## (e) DSH — Deutsche Sprachprüfung für den Hochschulzugang

*Provider:* individual universities (hochschulinterne Prüfung) following the KMK/HRK Rahmenordnung (RO-DT) and its Musterprüfungsordnung [S7]. Not centrally administered — exact timings vary slightly by university; the model regulation defines the standard values.

### Modules [S7][S8][S9]

Written exam — three Teilprüfungen (LV+WS form one joint paper):

| Part | Content | Duration | Weighting (within written) |
|---|---|---|---|
| HV — Hörverstehen | Lecture (heard twice); processing time 10 min after 1st reading + 40 min after 2nd reading (audio time not counted) | ~50 min processing + audio (total session typically ~80–85 min **[university-dependent]**) | 2 |
| LV — Leseverstehen | Academic text (model: 4,000–6,000 chars, may include graphic) + comprehension tasks | joint paper with WS: **90 min incl. reading time** | 2 |
| WS — Wissenschaftssprachliche Strukturen (grammar / wissenschaftliche Sprachkenntnisse) | Transform/paraphrase structures from the reading text (increasingly communicatively embedded tasks) | within the same 90-min paper | 1 |
| TP — Textproduktion | Argumentative essay ~250 words based on prompts (graphic, quotes) | 70 min | 2 |
| **Weighting ratio** | | | **HV : LV : WS : TP = 2 : 2 : 1 : 2** [S7] |

Monolingual DaF dictionaries permitted in all written parts [S7][S8].

Mündliche Prüfung (mandatory component; only skipped if the written exam was failed) [S7]:
- ~20 min preparation with dictionary + notes, then free short talk (~5 min) answering prompt questions followed by examiners' discussion (~15 min) before a commission (often 3 persons) [S8][S9]. Total ~35–40 min **[university-dependent]**.

### Scoring system [S7]
- Each part scored in %; written total = weighted percentage using the 2:2:1:2 ratio.
- **Pass rule: the exam is passed only if BOTH the written exam AND the oral exam reach ≥ 57 %.**
- Grade levels — each requires the threshold in **both** blocks:
  - **DSH-1:** ≥ 57 % in written and oral
  - **DSH-2:** ≥ 67 % in written and oral
  - **DSH-3:** ≥ 82 % in written and oral
- If one block lands lower, the lower level becomes the overall result [S8].
- DSH-2 counts as proof of language proficiency for admission/enrolment to ALL degree programmes [S7]; most programmes require DSH-2 [S10].

### Speaking format [S8][S9]
Individual exam (no pairs): prepared short presentation from a text/graphic related to the intended field of study, then unscripted discussion with the commission. Notes allowed during prep; reading aloud a written-out text not allowed.

### Recognition
A DSH passed per the RO-DT is recognized by **all** German universities as German-language proof for admission [S11]; one portal reports Stuttgart accepts only its own certificate [S10 — secondary claim, verify locally].

## Notes for LMS blueprint storage

1. **Goethe exams share one scoring engine**: store `scale_factor = 3.33`, `module_max = 100`, `pass_per_module = 60` once and reuse across B1/B2/C1; only part counts, item counts, and durations differ per exam/module.
2. **telc needs two pass gates** (`written_pass_pct = 60` over 166 pts, `oral_pass_pct = 60` over 48 pts) plus intra-written compensation = true.
3. **DSH needs dual-block thresholds** (57/67/82 applied to both blocks) and a non-uniform written weighting vector `[HV=2, LV=2, WS=1, TP=2]`; mark durations as configurable because they vary by host university.
4. Values flagged **[derived]** (telc 100-pt / 29-pt thresholds) are computed from official maxima and the officially described 60 %-per-block rule [S5][S6]; recompute automatically rather than hard-coding.

## Conflicts & caveats found during research

- germanexam.pro publishes telc C1 HS totals of "240 written / 75 oral" — inconsistent with telc's own published scoring table (166/48) [S5]; official document used.
- Pre-2025 Goethe C1 specs circulating online (Lesen 70 min, Schreiben 80 min, oral 15 min pair) are superseded by the 01.09.2025 edition used here [S3].
- DSH part durations differ between universities (e.g., HV session ~85 min Oldenburg [S8] vs ~80 min Münster [S9]); the Musterprüfungsordnung values (10+40 min processing, 90 min LV+WS, 70 min TP) are the normative baseline [S7].
