## 1. Overall format

Paper-based since 2001; digital version alongside. Both: 4 sections, scored independently, no overall grade; pure exam time **3h15min**; break after each section, longer break (20–60 min) between Hörverstehen and Schreiben [FAQ-D]; all four parts mandatory [FAQ-A].

| Section | Paper | Digital |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 3 texts · **30 items** · **60 min** [P] | 7 task types · **34 items** · **~55 min** [D] |
| Hören | 3 audios · **25 items** · **40 min** [P] | 7 task types · **30 items** · **~40 min** [D] |
| Schreiben | 1 task · **60 min** handwritten [P] | 2 tasks · **~60 min** typed [D] |
| Sprechen | 7 tasks · **35 min** per site [P] ⚠️ booklet: 30 min + 5 min Anleitung [MT01-MA] | 7 tasks · **~35 min** [D] |

## 2. Leseverstehen (paper)

60 min incl. 10 min transfer after min 50; only answer sheet counts [P][MT01-LV].

| Text | Type | Items | Format | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LT1 | Kurztexte (ads/course listings) | 1–10 | Zuordnung (persons→texts A–I; "I" = none fits; each text once) | ~10 min |
| LT2 | journalistischer Text (~85 lines) | 11–20 | MC A/B/C | ~20 min |
| LT3 | Fachzeitschrift-Text (~90 lines) | 21–30 | Ja / Nein / Text sagt dazu nichts | ~20 min |

Word counts not officially published — unverified; line counts measured from Modelltest PDF.

## 3. Hörverstehen (paper)

**Play rules (official):** "Die Texte 1 und 2 hören Sie einmal, den Text 3 hören Sie zweimal." [P][MT01-HV]

| Part | Type | Speakers | Plays | Items | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HT1 | Alltagsdialog (campus) | 2 | once | 1–8 | Kurzantwort (Stichwörter) |
| HT2 | Interview/Diskussion | 3 | once | 9–18 | Richtig/Falsch |
| HT3 | Expertengespräch | 2 | twice | 19–25 | Kurzantwort; 2nd pass → verify → 10 min transfer |

Audio durations not officially published — unverified.

## 4. Schriftlicher Ausdruck (paper)

One integrated task, 60 min (+~5 min instructions), handwritten; only answer sheet graded [P][MT01-SA].

**Official time split (from exam instructions):** Grafikbeschreibung **max 20 min** · Argumentation **max 40 min** [MT01-SA].

Task anatomy: theme + **two graphics** (trend + group comparison) → describe development → compare groups → take stance with justification → pros AND cons → home-country situation. Paper word minimum: none published (unverified). **Digital has official targets:** summary 100–150 words, argumentative essay ≥200 words [D].

## 5. Mündlicher Ausdruck — 7 Aufgaben (paper, official Modelltest 01 [MT01-MA])

Recorded via headset, no examiner present, rated centrally [P][FAQ-D]. Total 30 min + 5 min instructions.

| # | Scenario | Prep | Speak |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phone call (Hochschulsportzentrum): introduce, why calling, ask details | 0:30 | 0:30 |
| 2 | Private chat (cafeteria): problems in home country + countermeasures | 1:00 | 1:00 |
| 3 | German class: describe 1–2 graphics | 1:00 | 1:30 |
| 4 | Panel discussion: weigh pros/cons of project, justify stance | 3:00 | 2:00 |
| 5 | Advise friend (two options, e.g. private uni) | 2:00 | 1:30 |
| 6 | Seminar presentation with graph: causes + consequences from data | 3:00 | 2:00 |
| 7 | Informal advice (bus vs train) | 1:30 | 1:30 |

⚠️ Legacy prep sources cite Aufgabe 1 = 45 s; current official Modelltest = **30 s** — use 30 s.

## 6. Scoring

**Paper:** LV/HV = 1 point/item; **TDN thresholds re-calibrated per test form** [PAusw]:

| TDN | Lesen (max 30) | Hören (max 25) |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | ≥24 / 25 / 26 (per form) | ≥19 / 20 / 21 |
| 4 | ≥20 / 21 / 22 | ≥15 / 16 / 17 |
| 3 | ≥14 / 15 / 16 | ≥10 / 11 / 12 |

SA/MA: rated centrally by trained raters; in MA **each of the 7 tasks is itself mapped to a TDN level** (difficulty differs per task) [PAusw].

**Digital:** uniform **0–20 points per section** (total 0–80); identical bands for ALL sections [DAusw][Leipzig]:

| Unter TDN3 | TDN3 | TDN4 | TDN5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | 5–9 | 10–15 | 16–20 |

**Passing:** TDN 3 in all four parts = minimum certificate; **TDN 4 in all four** = standard university admission; TDN 5 all = qualifies for all programs [Leipzig][Goethe]. Certificate validity: **unlimited** [Home] (universities often prefer ≤2 years — common practice, not a rule).

## 7. Paper vs Digital — key deltas

- Reading: 3 long texts → 7 varied task types (cloze, paragraph ordering, speech-act matching, error spotting); forward-only
- Listening: 3 audios (HT3 twice) → 7 task types incl. **video** input, note-taking fields; forward-only
- Writing: 1 combined essay → **2 tasks** (summary 100–150 w + essay ≥200 w), typed
- Embedded unscored trial items add up to ~30 min [FAQ-D]
- Results: paper ~6 weeks, digital ~3 weeks [Termine] (RUB says 4 wks digital — minor conflict)
- **Combined certificate:** best section results from different digital sittings within **max 6 months** merge into one certificate [Home]
- Price Germany: paper €215 / digital €210 [FAQ-A][Hagen][RUB]
- 2026: 10 national dates — 6 digital + 4 paper [DKFA] (10 Feb paper, 03 Mar digital, 21 Apr digital, 19 May paper, 11 Jun digital, 09 Jul paper, 25 Aug digital, 22 Sep digital, 15 Oct paper, 12 Nov digital)
